======================================================================
  Kinect Virtual Camera - Required Driver Setup (Zadig)
======================================================================

IMPORTANT: FIRST-TIME SETUP REQUIREMENT
---------------------------------------
The Xbox 360 Kinect (Model 1414) does not use standard UVC webcam
drivers. Modern Windows 10 and 11 require Microsoft's built-in
WinUSB (winusb.sys) driver to communicate with the sensor securely.

Before this virtual camera can stream video, you MUST associate
WinUSB with the Kinect using the free, open-source Zadig utility.

STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS:
-------------------------
1. Plug your Kinect into 12V wall power and connect USB to your PC.
2. Download Zadig from: https://zadig.akeo.ie
   (If you leave the option checked on the final installer screen,
   your browser will open this page automatically).
3. Run Zadig, open the "Options" menu, and select "List All Devices".
4. From the dropdown list, select:
      "Xbox NUI Camera"  (USB ID: 045E 02AE)
5. Ensure the target driver on the right is set to "WinUSB".
6. Click "Replace Driver" (or "Install Driver").
7. (Optional - for Tilt & LED control):
   In the dropdown, select "Xbox NUI Motor" (USB ID: 045E 02B0)
   and replace its driver with "WinUSB" as well.

Once completed, the driver setup is permanent and plug-and-play!
You can now open OBS Studio, Discord, or the Kinect Control Panel.
